This policy explains how information is handled when you use GFX Tool.

Information and technology we use

GFX Tool uses analytics and advertising technology. These services may automatically receive information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app usage and interactions, and diagnostic data. The exact information depends on your device, settings, and the services in use.

How information is used

Analytics information helps understand app usage and improve the app. Advertising technology may use information to deliver ads, measure their performance, and help prevent fraud. Device settings may let you limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier.

Third-party services

Analytics and advertising services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is governed by those policies, not this one. Review the privacy information for the services available on your device and in your region.

Retention and choices

Information collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. You can manage certain data and advertising preferences through your device settings. Uninstalling GFX Tool stops its collection of app usage information from that device, but does not delete information already held by a service provider.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, with the effective date updated above.

Contact
